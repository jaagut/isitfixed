# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for
the project and its users. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information,
and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting are not acceptable.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is available at the link above and applies to all project spaces
(issues, pull requests, discussions) and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer, [Jan Gutsche](https://github.com/jaagut). All
reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits,
code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors for repeated or severe violations.
